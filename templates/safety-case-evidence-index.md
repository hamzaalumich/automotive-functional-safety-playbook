## Evidence review rules

- Each evidence item supports a specific claim or requirement.
- Configuration is uniquely identified.
- Acceptance criteria are controlled.
- Limitations and anomalies are visible.
- Superseded evidence is not credited for the release.
- Supplier evidence includes assumptions of use and integration obligations.
